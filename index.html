The problem

Buying a plant is easy. Keeping it alive isn’t.

Beginner plant owners juggle social media, stores, search engines, and memory. Every step works on its own; the journey does not.

What if the product that helps someone choose a plant could keep supporting them after the purchase?

Discovery

From “I want a plant” to “this plant is right for me.”

Confidence

Decision-focused details before committing.

Care

Routines and reminders, not static articles.

Recovery

Problem → understanding → action → recovery.

Full problem statement and solution

Plant lovers need a simpler, more confident way to discover plants that fit their lifestyle, purchase them with clear expectations, and receive ongoing support. Nabta unifies discovery, purchasing, personalized care, and education in one experience, supporting users through every stage of ownership.

Key insight

Ownership is the product. Shopping is a step.

Journey mapping flipped the initial assumption. The purchase became a transition point, not the destination.

Testing & results

What users taught me.

Discovery

Users explored before searching → raised exploration visibility.

Plant details

Users scanned for care info → added a care summary.

Checkout

Users checked the total → transparent breakdown.

My Plants

Expected purchases to appear → linked order completion to ownership.

Diagnosis

Unsure where to start → “Something’s Wrong” entry point.

Strong products connect the right experiences at the right moment: purchase → ownership → care → recovery.
What I would measure next

Recommendation interaction, add-to-cart and checkout completion, plant setup and reminder creation, diagnosis completion, repeat visits. These are future measures unless validated in a live product.
